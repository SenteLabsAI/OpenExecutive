# Tallgrass Studio — Overview

Tallgrass Studio is an independent brand and product design studio run by one
person: Maya Lindqvist, founder and principal designer. There is no team.
Maya sells the work, does the work, invoices for it, and decides what the
studio takes on next. A contract illustrator helps with overflow on some
projects but never talks to clients.

## What the studio sells

Since September 2026, new clients buy fixed-fee sprints instead of hours:

| Sprint | Price | Length | What the founder gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand Sprint | $8,500 | 2 weeks | Logo, type, color, a one-page brand guide |
| Site Sprint | $11,000 | 3 weeks | Marketing site designed and built in Webflow |
| UI Refresh | $9,500 | 2–3 weeks | Visual refresh of up to 12 product screens |

Existing clients stay on their hourly terms until their current projects end.
One inbound lead (Northwind Labs) has asked to stay on hourly billing; Maya has
not answered yet.

## Clients and pipeline

- Two active clients: Brightwell (Site Sprint, milestone 2 of 3) and Oda Health
  (UI Refresh, week 1).
- Brightwell's first invoice ($5,500) is 41 days old and unpaid.
- Five leads in the pipeline, three of them referrals from past clients.

## The areas Maya tracks

Maya groups her goals into four areas — strategy, finance, marketing and
product. They are not departments; they are how one person keeps the parts of
the business in view.

## Rhythm

- Client work Tuesday to Thursday; sales, admin and the template kit on Monday
  and Friday.
- A planned month off in December, only if the cash buffer reaches three months
  of expenses by November 30.
